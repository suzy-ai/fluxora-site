Why I built it

Built by a hygienist, for the whole office

I'm a registered dental hygienist. I've seen how much of a front-desk day goes to phone tag, re-typing paper forms and chasing recalls, and how little of it is about the patient standing at the counter.

Fluxora is my answer: a tool that handles the repetitive parts so your team can spend its time on insurance, treatment coordination and patient care. It's being shaped with real front-desk teams, one office at a time.

Questions

Common questions

Do we have to stop using Dentrix (or our current software)?

No. Fluxora runs alongside it. You bring your patient list over with the export file your software already makes, and keep charting and billing where they are today.

What does it cost?

Pilot pricing will be shared with waitlist offices first. There's no cost to join the waitlist or to do the 30-minute walkthrough.

Is our patient information safe?

Patient data is encrypted and every access is logged. Pilots start with made-up test patients, and no real patient information is used until our HIPAA review is complete and a Business Associate Agreement is signed with your office.

Where are you starting?

With a small group of independent offices in Southern California, so we can visit in person and set things up properly.

Does the AI send texts to patients on its own?

No. It suggests and drafts. Your team reviews and sends.
